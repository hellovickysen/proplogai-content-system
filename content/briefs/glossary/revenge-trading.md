# Content Brief: Revenge Trading

## Primary question and promise

Answer **“What is revenge trading?”** immediately. Explain that it is a post-loss decision pattern in which recovering the lost money becomes the purpose of the next trade and part of the written plan changes or is ignored.

## Reader problem

The current glossary correction is already clear and approved with the practical article. J16 should preserve the `$50` XAUUSD example and the five decision checks while giving the glossary its own current keyword evidence, complete source coverage and a sharper boundary from the longer article.

## Required teaching path

1. Keep the two-sentence definition and say that another trade after a loss is not automatically revenge trading.
2. Keep one fictional XAUUSD London-session example: a planned Asian-range liquidity sweep and confirmed breakout ends at `−$50`; three minutes later, the trader wants to enter before fresh confirmation and uses a bigger size to recover the loss.
3. Keep the five checks in a visible ordered list: setup, confirmation, session, size and reason.
4. State the decision test in simple words: would you take this same setup, with the same confirmation and size, if the previous result were `$0`?
5. Separate revenge trading from tilt and overtrading. Tilt is a broader pressure state; overtrading is a wider process change; revenge trading is the narrower recovery-after-loss purpose.
6. Explain what happens next without inventing a universal cooldown: follow the loss-response rule written before the session and the current rule sheet for the exact prop-firm program and stage.
7. End with one record: previous result, urge noticed, setup and confirmation, size method, reason and final decision.

## Safety and evidence boundaries

- Do not label every trade after a loss as revenge trading.
- Do not claim a loss, frustration or emotion caused the next decision; compare the written plan with the recorded decision.
- Do not prescribe a universal waiting time, number of losses, trade limit or risk percentage.
- Do not diagnose a trader or claim that revenge trading always causes another loss.
- Do not say that PropLogAI detects, prevents or blocks revenge trading.
