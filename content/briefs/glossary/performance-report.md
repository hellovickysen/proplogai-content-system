# Content Brief: Trading Performance Report

## Primary question and promise

Answer **“What is a trading performance report?”** immediately. Explain that it is a summary of trades from one clearly defined period, built from records the trader can check. It may combine results, the account path, setup or session breakdowns, and process notes.

## Reader problem

The current page lists many scores and implies that PropLogAI generates a broad AI report. That wording is not supported by the approved product source register. It also risks making profit and loss look like proof of discipline. The rewritten page must show how a trader reads a report and checks each conclusion against the underlying trades.

## Evidence and SEO decision

- Keep `/glossary/performance-report`; it is the established definition owner and no migration is justified.
- Semrush returned no usable exact-phrase volume for `trading performance report` in India.
- Semrush estimated India volume 170 for the broader `trading report`, but visible meanings were mixed and do not justify changing the primary keyword or URL.

- `RES-020` supports using account history, result summaries, balance/equity paths, symbols and risk information in a trading report.
- `RES-021` supports explaining the calculation method and what is included or excluded, and warning that past results do not predict future results.
- Product claims must stay within the approved logging, dashboard and rule/emotion-recording statements in `PLAI-002`, `PLAI-003` and `PLAI-005`.

## Required teaching path

1. Define the report and state that the date range, account, currency, trade count and counting method must be named.
2. Separate two evidence groups:
   - results: net P&L, win rate, average win/loss, expectancy, profit factor, drawdown and equity curve;
   - recorded process: setup, session, planned risk, rule-following answer, emotion tag and written note.
3. State plainly that profit or loss does not prove whether the trader followed the plan.
4. Use one fictional report for 20 XAUUSD trades across a defined month. Keep it consistent with the approved metric sample: 8 wins, 12 losses, $1,440 gross profit, $960 gross loss and +$480 net result before any separately stated cost treatment.
5. Show a simple session split using Asian, London and New York sessions, and a setup split using breakout and liquidity sweep. Label all added counts and results as fictional and make their totals reconcile.
6. Show how logged process data answers a different question: for example, `15 trades marked plan followed`, `5 marked plan not followed`. Do not call this a discipline score or infer it from P&L.
7. Show a safe review conclusion: identify one recorded pattern to inspect in the original trades, such as three of the five plan-not-followed entries occurring outside the planned session. Do not prescribe a universal trade limit or setup rule.
8. Explain that weekly, monthly or challenge-stage reports are all possible; the right period depends on the question and must remain consistent when comparing reports.
9. Explain the checks before trusting a report: missing trades, duplicate entries, grouped partial exits, breakeven treatment, commission, swap, spread, deposits, withdrawals and open positions.
10. End with the reader action: open the original records behind one surprising number before changing the plan.

## Safety and evidence boundaries

- Do not claim that PropLogAI automatically generates monthly AI reports, psychology analysis, discipline scores or personalised action plans without an approved product source.
- Do not score discipline from P&L, win rate or any other financial result.
- Do not turn a fictional pattern into a rule or recommendation for every trader.
- Do not say that a past report predicts the next period.
- State the sample, units, costs and counting method before comparisons.
