# Content Brief: Loss Aversion

## Primary question and promise

Answer **“What is loss aversion in trading?”** immediately. Explain that a possible loss can influence a decision more strongly than an equivalent gain, while making clear that one trade cannot prove a psychological bias.

## Reader problem

The current definition is safe but abstract. A trader may recognise the behaviour only when the page shows a concrete decision: closing a planned winner early because giving back profit feels uncomfortable, or changing a planned exit because recording the loss feels painful. The page must help the reader review the decision without diagnosing them.

## Evidence and SEO decision

- Keep `/glossary/loss-aversion`; it is the established definition owner and no migration is justified.
- Semrush India reports `n/a` for exact local volume and KD; global volume is 60. Treat the India keyword as an editorial hypothesis.
- GSC returned 0 clicks and 0 impressions for queries containing `loss aversion` during 6 July–2 October 2026.
- `RES-001` supports the general prospect-theory concept that losses and gains can be valued differently. It does not prove a fixed multiplier, diagnose a trader or explain one isolated trade.

## Required teaching path

1. Define loss aversion in one simple sentence.
2. Explain that it concerns a decision under uncertainty, not the fact that a trader dislikes losing.
3. Use a fictional XAUUSD example with a written London-session liquidity-sweep plan. Show the planned exit, the later uncomfortable feeling and the actual changed action.
4. Separate three possibilities: a rule-based response to new market information, an emotional change without a new rule-based reason, and an unknown cause that needs more journal evidence.
5. Tell the reader to compare several similar trades before naming a pattern.
6. Show what to record: planned exit, actual exit, new information, feeling, reason written at the time and whether the plan was followed.
7. State that loss aversion does not mean a trader should ignore valid new information or mechanically hold every trade.
8. Link to the emotions guide for the review workflow and to Stop Loss or Risk Per Trade only where those terms need definition.

## Safety and evidence boundaries

- Do not use a universal `losses feel twice as painful` claim.
- Do not diagnose loss aversion from one trade or emotion tag.
- Do not promise that a checklist or journal will remove the bias.
- Do not turn the example into trade advice or a recommended XAUUSD exit.
- Use USD for the fictional trading amounts.
