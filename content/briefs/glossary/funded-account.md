# Content Brief: Funded Trading Account

## Primary question and promise

Answer **“What is a funded trading account?”** immediately. Explain that, in online prop trading, it is an account stage offered under a firm's agreement after its eligibility conditions are met. The word `funded` does not by itself tell the trader whether the account uses simulated or live capital.

## Reader problem

The current definition contains the correct warning but does not make the practical check memorable. A trader may pass a challenge, see a large account balance and assume that the full amount is live money assigned to them. The revision must separate four things: the product label, the account model, the rules that remain active and the conditions for a monetary reward.

- Current first-party evidence confirms the need for the warning: FTMO says its FTMO Account is a demo account with fictitious capital and no client trades on live markets; FundedNext describes its post-challenge stage as a simulated funded account with reward eligibility.

## Required teaching path

1. Define the term in two short sentences and state the simulated-versus-live check immediately.
2. Explain the four separate questions:
   1. What does the firm call this stage?
   2. Is the account simulated, live or a model with possible later trade replication?
   3. Which loss and trading rules still apply?
   4. How is a reward or payout earned and requested?
3. Use one fictional XAUUSD example: after passing an evaluation, the trader receives a `$100,000 funded account` label. That label alone does not prove that $100,000 in live capital was transferred to the trader; the agreement and official account-model page decide what it means.
4. Add two current named examples, visibly dated `Checked 8 October 2026`:
   - FTMO says FTMO Accounts use fictitious capital in a simulated environment, while eligible rewards are based on the results under the product conditions;
   - FundedNext says traders who complete a challenge receive a simulated funded account and may earn rewards under its rules.
5. Explain that the trader may still have daily/overall loss rules, prohibited practices, reward cycles, open-position conditions or other account-specific requirements.
6. Explain the practical difference between balance and reward: a displayed account balance is not automatically withdrawable money; the agreement defines how performance may create a monetary reward.
7. End with one action: read the account-model section and reward rules before relying on the word `funded`.

## Firm and date boundaries

- Do not claim that all prop-firm funded accounts are simulated or that all are live. Use only the named current examples.
- Keep FTMO CFDs separate from FTMO Futures, which now uses distinct Sim-Funded and invitation-only Live Funded stages.
- Do not imply that a displayed balance belongs to the trader or can be withdrawn.
- Do not quote a universal profit split, payout time, scaling rule, breach consequence or account size.
- Recheck named account-model and reward statements when their source-register review date is reached.
- Do not recommend a firm or treat marketing language as proof of account structure.
