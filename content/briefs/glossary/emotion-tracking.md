# Content Brief: Emotion Tracking

## Primary question and promise

Answer **“What is emotion tracking in trading?”** immediately. Explain that it means recording a short feeling tag beside a specific trading moment and the observable decision that followed. The record provides context; it does not diagnose the trader or prove that the feeling caused the result.

## Reader problem

The current glossary definition is directionally correct but too light for the PropLogAI standard. A trader can see suggested labels, yet the page does not show a complete before/during/after record, approved sources, a visual note or the difference between feeling, urge, action and P&L. J17 should make the term immediately usable without repeating the longer guides.

## Required teaching path

1. Define emotion tracking in two simple sentences and separate the feeling from the decision and result.
2. Give five plain tags: calm, hesitant, frustrated, rushed and confident. Say traders may use their own short words consistently.
3. Show the minimum useful record in an ordered list: time and session, feeling, urge, planned condition, observable action, and result kept in a separate field.
4. Use one fictional XAUUSD London-session example. After a `−$35` trade, the trader records `frustrated`, feels an urge to recover the loss, and enters before the planned breakout candle closes. The useful record is `frustrated + urge to recover + entered before confirmation`; do not write `frustration caused the loss`.
5. Explain when to record it: once before entry, when an urge or management decision changes, and after exit. Keep each entry short enough to use consistently.
6. Explain how to review it: compare several similar records with setup, session, size, rule adherence and P&L. A repeated association is a question to investigate, not proof of cause.
7. End with one action: record one feeling and one observable decision on the next reviewed trade.

## Safety and evidence boundaries

- Do not diagnose anxiety, addiction, depression or another mental-health condition.
- Do not claim that a feeling caused a profit, loss or rule break.
- Do not label a trader as emotional from one record.
- Do not tell the reader to suppress every feeling or promise that journaling will remove emotion, prevent mistakes or create profit.
- Keep process quality separate from the final P&L. A planned loss and an unplanned win must remain possible in the explanation.
- Do not say PropLogAI senses or infers an emotion; the tag is entered by the trader.
