# Content Brief: Pre-Market Routine

## Primary question and promise

Answer **“What is a pre-market routine in trading?”** immediately. Define it as a short sequence completed before the trader’s planned session so the setup, account rules, risk and no-trade conditions are written before price movement creates pressure.

## Reader problem

The current page says a routine shifts the brain into professional execution, guarantees that nothing critical is missed, prescribes fixed minute blocks, uses a universal `7/10` focus rule, claims routines cause fewer impulsive decisions and says PropLogAI correlates preparation habits with outcomes. A beginner needs a plain preparation example, a clear difference between a routine, plan and checklist, and no promise that preparation guarantees discipline or a result.

## Required teaching path

1. Define a pre-market routine in two short sentences.
2. Explain that “pre-market” means before the trader’s planned session, not necessarily before an exchange opens. For an Indian forex trader, that could mean preparing before the London or New York session and recording time in IST.
3. Use one fictional XAUUSD London-session example:
   - check the relevant economic calendar and the exact current prop-firm rules for the planned window;
   - copy the planned session and XAUUSD setup into the journal;
   - mark the Asian high and low only because this setup uses them;
   - write the required breakout close, retest confirmation and invalidation;
   - write the planned USD loss, stop basis and no-trade condition;
   - save the checklist before considering an entry.
4. Distinguish the terms:
   - **Trading plan:** the wider personal framework for what, when, why and how much to trade;
   - **Pre-market routine:** the preparation sequence completed before the planned session;
   - **Checklist:** the short set of conditions checked for one considered decision.
5. Explain that the routine should match the trader’s strategy and firm rules. A trader who does not use Asian-session levels should not add them only because the example does.
6. State that completing the routine does not guarantee a valid setup, rule compliance, profit or emotional control. It creates a record that can be checked later.
7. End with one action: write a five-step routine for the next planned session using the trader’s own setup and account rules.

## Safety and evidence boundaries

- Remove the fixed `2/5/5/5/1 minute` schedule and the universal `7/10` focus rule.
- Do not say that a routine shifts the brain, creates professional execution, prevents impulsive decisions or guarantees that nothing is missed.
- Do not prescribe specific technical levels, timeframes, news filters, risk percentages or session hours as universal rules.
- Do not claim PropLogAI correlates habits with outcomes, detects readiness or decides whether the trader should trade.
- Keep the example fictional, in USD, with IST used only for recording context and no live signal.
