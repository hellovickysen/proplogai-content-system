# Content Brief: Prop Firm Expense Tracking

## Primary question and promise

Show one trader how to record every real prop-firm payment, refund, and payout in a simple USD log that remains useful later. The article should make the workflow easy enough to start immediately without turning it into tax, accounting, or investment advice.

## Reader problem

The reader remembers the latest payout or challenge result but may have earlier purchases, renewals, activation costs, refunds, and pending payouts spread across emails, statements, dashboards, and memory. The existing article mixes record keeping with unsupported typical prices and prescriptive ROI decisions.

## Required teaching path

1. Open with the gap between remembering a payout and remembering every earlier payment.
2. Give the one-payment-equals-one-row answer near the top.
3. Provide a one-row interactive builder and blank downloadable CSV.
4. Explain categories without calling any price or category universal.
5. Explain the minimum fields: date, firm, account or attempt, record type, category, USD amount, status, evidence, and note.
6. Show a clearly fictional multi-row example with no claim that its amounts are typical.
7. Give a calm monthly review checklist.
8. Separate record keeping from ROI calculation and trading-process review.
9. Explain the approved PropLogAI expense-and-payout workflow without calling it accounting or verified financial reporting.
10. Answer practical FAQs including failed challenges, pending payouts, USD versus INR, tax limits, and review frequency.
