# Content Brief: Prop Firm Trading Journal Pillar

## Primary question and promise

Show one trader what to record before, during, and after a prop-firm trade, then show how to turn those entries into one useful weekly lesson. The article must feel like a trader calmly explaining a repeatable journal routine to another trader.

## Reader problem

You may start a journal after a bad trading day, write detailed notes for several days, and then stop because the process feels like extra work. You may also record only entry, exit, and profit or loss, which does not explain why you took the trade or whether you followed your plan.

Use a concrete XAUUSD example with the London or New York session and a named setup such as a liquidity sweep or breakout. Explain each trading term in plain English and link its glossary definition when one exists.

## Required teaching path

1. Open with the moment after an XAUUSD trade when the trader remembers the result but cannot clearly explain the decision.
2. Give the direct answer: a useful journal records the plan, the execution, the result, and the lesson.
3. Explain the minimum before-trade fields: instrument, session, setup, direction, planned risk, entry reason, stop, and invalidation.
4. Explain the after-trade fields: result in dollars and R, execution quality, rule adherence, emotion, screenshot, and one-sentence lesson.
5. Show one fictional XAUUSD London-session liquidity-sweep or breakout example with internally consistent prices and USD amounts.
6. Separate a good process trade from a profitable trade. A loss can follow the plan; a win can still contain a rule break.
7. Teach a three-minute daily entry so the journal stays usable.
8. Show a simple weekly review: repeated setup, repeated mistake, strongest habit, weakest session, and one change for next week.
9. Explain when the fields guide, template, P&L calendar, weekly review, and funded-account workflow should be used instead of repeating their full content.
10. Explain how PropLogAI can organise user-entered trades, screenshots, emotions, rule-adherence notes, metrics, and calendar results without claiming broker verification, automatic imports, signals, or guaranteed improvement.
