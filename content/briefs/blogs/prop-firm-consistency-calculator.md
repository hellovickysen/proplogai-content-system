# Content Brief: Consistency Rule Calculator

## Primary question and promise

Help one trader calculate a best-day consistency percentage, understand why a losing day can raise it, and compare it with the exact rule copied from the current program. Make clear that the result is arithmetic rather than official account or payout status.

## Reader problem

The trader may have positive total profit but still see an unexpected consistency percentage. They may feel pushed to take more trades to “fix” the number. The current article encourages continued trading, presents an unsupported common threshold range, treats one formula as general, and claims automatic PropLogAI consistency tracking without approved feature evidence.

Use a fictional five-day XAUUSD record with named London and New York setups. Speak directly to the trader in simple English and explain every rule term in context.
