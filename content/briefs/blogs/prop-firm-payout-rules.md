# Content Brief: Prop Firm Payout Rules

## Primary question and promise

Show one trader how to check whether a payout request can be submitted under one exact prop-firm program. The reader should finish with a plain-English payout sheet covering the account stage, chosen payout path, profit and day conditions, consistency rule, open-position rule, profit share, request limit, payment method, account impact, and current source-check date.

## Reader problem

You may see profit in a funded-stage dashboard and assume it is ready to withdraw. The request may still depend on a trading cycle, profitable days, consistency, closed positions, account review, identity checks, a minimum amount, or a program-specific payout path.

Passing an evaluation, showing profit, becoming eligible, submitting a request, receiving approval, and receiving money are separate events. The article must make those stages easy to understand without claiming that any firm will approve or pay a request.

## Search angle and format

This is not a list of the best or fastest-paying firms. It is a request-readiness workflow:

**Name the exact funded-stage program and payout path, check each condition, calculate the request in USD, record what the withdrawal changes, and keep the official decision separate from your estimate.**

Use one clearly fictional `$100,000` XAUUSD funded-stage account for the main walkthrough. Named firms appear only in a small dated comparison proving that payout paths differ.

## Required teaching path

1. Open with a trader seeing profit in the dashboard and assuming the money is ready to withdraw.
2. Give the direct answer: profit shown is not the same as payout eligibility or money received.
3. Identify the exact firm, program, funded stage, payout path, purchase or reset date, account size, and rule-check date.
4. Separate the six stages: profit shown, condition met, request available, request submitted, request approved, money received.
5. Explain common condition categories without universal numbers: trading cycle, profitable days, consistency, minimum growth, closed positions, account compliance, KYC, and country or method availability.
6. Explain gross account profit, trader share, fees, and received amount with one fictional USD calculation.
7. Show how a withdrawal may change balance, drawdown buffer, consistency window, winning-day count, or account status depending on the exact rule.
8. Use one fictional `$100,000` XAUUSD funded-stage example with no suggested trading target.
9. Explain three outcomes: eligible to request, not yet eligible but account active, and request under review or not approved.
10. Give a before-request checklist and an after-request record checklist.
11. Explain how PropLogAI can store user-entered payout status, amount, evidence, fees, and received date without calculating official eligibility or verifying payment.
