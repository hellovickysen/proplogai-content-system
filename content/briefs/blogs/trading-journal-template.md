# Content Brief: Trading Journal Template for Prop Firm Traders

## Primary question and article promise

Give the reader a copyable trading-journal template they can use immediately. Explain what belongs in each section, how to keep the record practical, and how to review process and rule adherence separately from profit or loss.

The page must satisfy template intent. It cannot be a renamed version of the broad journal guide or a long field catalogue.

## Reader problem

The reader knows they should keep a trading journal but does not know how to structure one. Existing templates often focus on entries, exits, and P&L while leaving out account context, planned risk, rule adherence, emotional state, screenshots, and a short review step.

## Firm/program variation and formula assumptions

- Do not state a universal risk percentage, drawdown limit, consistency rule, or required field.
- Account-rule fields are user-entered references to the trader's own current firm/program rules.
- Do not calculate eligibility, payout status, or breach status.
- The fictional example must use concrete market language—XAUUSD, Asian session range, London session, liquidity sweep, and breakout confirmation—while avoiding a named firm, entry signal, or prescriptive price and risk values.
