# Content Brief: Prop Firm Rules Guide

## Primary question and promise

Show one trader how to read the rules of one exact prop-firm program before paying for a challenge. The reader should finish with a one-page rule sheet covering the account, phase, loss limits, profit target, day definition, trading restrictions, consistency conditions, and payout conditions that apply to that program.

## Reader problem

You may read the large percentage on a sales page and assume you understand the challenge. Later, you discover that open P&L counts toward a daily limit, the trading day resets in another time zone, a consistency condition applies only at payout, or a restriction changes between the challenge and funded stage.

The article must help you slow down and write the exact rule before you pay. It must not recommend a firm, rank firms, or imply that passing is likely.

## Search angle and format

This is not a list of rules copied from several firms. It is a method for reading any one program safely:

**Name the exact program and phase, write each rule in plain English, calculate one USD example, and record what happens when the condition is missed.**

Use a fictional `$100,000` XAUUSD challenge rule sheet for the main walkthrough. The fictional sheet must be clearly labelled and internally consistent. Named firms appear only in a small dated comparison that proves rules can differ.

## Required teaching path

1. Open with a trader about to pay for a challenge after reading only the headline target and account size.
2. Give the direct answer: read the exact program, phase, and current official rule page before paying.
3. Explain the identity block: firm, program, phase, account size, platform, server time zone, and rule-page check date.
4. Explain loss rules separately: daily loss, overall or maximum loss, static versus trailing, balance versus equity, open P&L, commissions, swaps, and reset time.
5. Explain progress conditions separately: profit target, minimum days, time limit, consistency condition, and what happens when the condition is not met.
6. Explain trading restrictions: news, holding overnight or over weekends, copy trading, prohibited strategies, lot or contract limits, and inactivity. Tell the reader to record only restrictions published for the exact program.
7. Explain challenge-stage versus funded-stage and payout-stage differences.
8. Show a fictional `$100,000` XAUUSD rule sheet and one London-session loss-buffer example in USD.
9. Show the three consequence types clearly: immediate breach, trading pause or restriction, and condition not yet met.
10. Give a before-payment checklist and a before-session checklist.
11. Explain how PropLogAI can store the trader's manual notes, trade results, screenshots, rule-adherence records, and P&L calendar without replacing the firm's dashboard or official rules.
