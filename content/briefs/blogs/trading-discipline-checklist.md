# Content Brief: Trading Discipline Checklist for Prop Firm Traders

## Primary question and promise

Answer one practical question: **What should you check before the session, before an entry, after a loss and at the end of the day?**

Give the trader a checklist that can be copied into a notebook, spreadsheet or journal and completed without reading a long explanation during the session. The checklist does not choose a trade, predict an outcome or create universal risk rules. It helps the trader compare the next decision with their own written plan and the current official rules for their exact prop-firm program.

## Reader problem

You may know your setup but still feel rushed when XAUUSD moves during the London session. You may remember the entry condition and forget the account state, confirmation, normal sizing method or stop rule. After a loss, you may change the plan without noticing it.

The page should speak directly to that moment. The trader should be able to open the page, run the relevant section and record a clear `Yes`, `No` or `Not checked` answer.

## Direct opening

Open with a specific moment:

> XAUUSD moves above the Asian-session high during London. You recognise your breakout setup and feel ready to enter. Before you click, can you confirm the setup, candle close, account state, normal size and reason without changing your rules?

Then give the quick answer:

> Use four short checks: before the session, before the trade, after a loss or strong emotion, and at the end of the day. Mark each required item `Yes`, `No` or `Not checked`. A required `No` means the decision does not match your current written plan. The checklist does not tell you whether the trade will win.

## Required XAUUSD example

Use one fictional USD example across the article:

1. The trader plans to trade XAUUSD only during the London session.
2. The named setup is an Asian-session liquidity sweep followed by a breakout candle close.
3. The trader checks the exact current firm dashboard and records the remaining account buffer shown there. Do not invent a universal buffer or safe amount.
4. Price moves before the breakout candle closes. `Setup present` may be `Yes`, while `Required confirmation complete` is `No`.
5. The checklist result is `Wait`. It is not a sell or buy signal; the trader's own required confirmation is incomplete.
6. Later, a fresh setup either passes every required personal check or is skipped.

This example teaches how to read the checklist. It does not recommend XAUUSD, a direction, a session, a setup, a risk percentage or a lot size.

## Required checklist structure

### 1. Before the session

- Exact firm, program, account stage and dashboard checked.
- Allowed instrument and planned session recorded.
- Named setups for today recorded.
- Personal sizing method and loss-response rule available.
- Any current official restriction relevant to the account checked at its source.
- Physical state or distraction that the trader personally treats as a stop condition recorded without diagnosis.

### 2. Before the trade

- Instrument and session match the plan.
- Named setup is present.
- Required confirmation is complete.
- Invalidation or stop condition is written before entry.
- Size comes from the normal documented method.
- Current account state remains inside the exact official limits.
- Reason can be explained without `FOMO`, recovery or outcome language.

### 3. After a loss, missed move or strong urge

- Freeze the next entry while recording the facts.
- Ask: `Would I take this exact trade if the last result were $0?`
- Check whether setup, confirmation, session, size or reason changed.
- Apply the loss-response rule written before the session.
- Require a completely new valid setup if the plan and current firm rules allow another trade.

Do not prescribe a universal cooldown, consecutive-loss rule, maximum trade count, smaller size or stop-for-the-day instruction. Those conditions must come from the trader's own prewritten plan and exact official account rules.

### 4. End of day

- Planned trades and actual trades recorded separately.
- Profit or loss recorded in USD but not used as the discipline score.
- Rule adherence, setup compliance, emotion or urge, and screenshots recorded.
- One decision that followed the plan and one decision that changed are identified.
- Any proposed rule change is saved for later review instead of being rewritten immediately after one result.

## Checklist result language

Use only these outcomes:

- **Ready for your own decision:** every required personal and account check is complete. This is not a signal and does not predict a win.
- **Wait:** a required confirmation or factual check is incomplete.
- **Stop and review your plan:** a required personal rule or current official account condition is not met.

Never use `safe trade`, `high probability`, `approved trade`, `take the trade`, `guaranteed`, or a score that implies the market decision is good.
