# Trade Review

A trade review is the check you make after a trade: compare the plan with the actual execution, confirm the result and write one factual observation.

## What is a trade review?

A trade review is a post-trade check. You compare the written plan with what you did, confirm the entry, exit, costs and result, then write one evidence-based observation.

**A profit does not prove that you followed the plan. A loss does not prove that the decision was wrong.**

## Simple XAUUSD trade review

1. **Plan:** Wait for the Asian high to break and for a candle to close above it.
2. **Execution:** Entered after the close, used the planned stop and made no unplanned change.
3. **Record check:** Entry, exit, stated costs and the +$85 result match the account history.
4. **One observation:** The written conditions and recorded action matched in this trade. That does not prove the setup will work next time.

If the trader entered before the close and made **+$120**, the review should call it a **profitable rule break**. The result was positive, while setup compliance was incomplete.

## What should you check?

1. Is the journal entry complete?
2. Did the planned setup and confirmation appear?
3. Did the entry, exit, stop, size or reason change?
4. Do the execution, costs and result match the account history?
5. What one factual note belongs in a later multi-trade review?

## Trade review vs performance report

A trade review checks one completed decision. A trading performance report summarises a defined group of trades.

## Trade review vs weekly or monthly review

A single-trade review can happen after the trade or at a consistent later time. Weekly and monthly reviews compare several entries. There is no universal five-minute, thirty-minute or one-hour schedule every trader must follow.

## What should you do next?

Open one completed journal entry and mark it **plan matched**, **plan changed** or **record incomplete**. Then write one factual sentence.
