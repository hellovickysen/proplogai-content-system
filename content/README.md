# Content Workspace

- `briefs/blogs/` and `briefs/glossary/` hold approved briefs.
- `drafts/blogs/` and `drafts/glossary/` hold generated work awaiting review.
- `approved/blogs/` and `approved/glossary/` hold exact human-approved revisions.
- `published/` may hold snapshots and final URLs after a future publishing integration.

Use the same item ID and slug across brief, draft, approval, and audit records. Never overwrite an approved revision.
