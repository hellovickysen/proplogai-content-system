# Audit One Content Item

1. Identify URL, content type, intended reader, and likely intent.
2. Check the live inventory for duplicates and related pages.
3. Capture metadata, canonical behaviour, main copy, links, and schema.
4. Verify sensitive claims and source freshness.
5. Score the page with the quality standard.
6. Produce `templates/audit-report.md`.
7. Add confirmed remediation work to the refresh queue.

Do not rewrite or publish during an audit-only task.
