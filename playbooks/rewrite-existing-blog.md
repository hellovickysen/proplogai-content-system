## Objective

Transform an existing ProplogAI blog into the highest-quality beginner-friendly educational article.

## Inputs

- Existing blog
- Audit report

---

## Workflow

1. Read the audit report.
2. Identify every improvement.
3. Preserve the core message.
4. Rewrite for beginners.
5. Improve formatting.
6. Add glossary opportunities.
7. Add visual opportunities.
8. Add interactive opportunities.
9. Validate with checklist.
10. Return final article.

---

## Deliverables

- Final rewritten article
- Change summary
- Remaining recommendations

---

## Completion Criteria

The rewritten article scores at least 90/100 against the Quality Standard.
