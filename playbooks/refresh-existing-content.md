# Refresh Existing Content

1. Audit the live page and capture its current canonical version.
2. Identify why a refresh is needed: factual drift, product drift, weak intent match, poor readability, broken links, overlap, or missing value.
3. Preserve correct sections and URLs where possible.
4. Create a refresh brief with old and proposed intent.
5. Re-verify every time-sensitive claim and link.
6. Draft a new revision without overwriting the approved or published snapshot.
7. Run full SEO, fact, QA, and human review.
8. Record redirects or consolidation instructions separately.
