# Blog Pre-Publish Checklist

## Reader value

- [ ] The main question is answered near the top.
- [ ] The article is understandable to a Grade 6-8 reader.
- [ ] The example states its assumptions.
- [ ] Process quality is separate from trade outcome.
- [ ] Limitations and a practical next step are visible.

## Accuracy and safety

- [ ] Every sensitive claim cites a source-register ID in the brief or review notes.
- [ ] Product and research claims are `Approved`; named firm rules are `Verified`.
- [ ] No cited row is past its `review_by` date.
- [ ] No `Needs Fact Check` or `Prohibited` wording remains in the draft.
- [ ] No signal, forecast, personalised risk instruction, or profit promise appears.
- [ ] No rule is presented as universal when firms differ.
- [ ] Formulas and calculations were checked deterministically.

## SEO and operations

- [ ] Intent, canonical owner, title, description, slug, and H1 align.
- [ ] Cannibalisation and content role are recorded.
- [ ] Internal and glossary links are useful and verified.
- [ ] Overall QA is at least 90; accuracy and safety are at least 9/10.
- [ ] A human approved the exact revision and hash.
