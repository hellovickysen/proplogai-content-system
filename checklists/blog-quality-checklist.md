# Blog Quality Checklist

Use after drafting, SEO editing, and fact-checking. Score each category from 0–10 and record evidence for every score. Scores below 9 require correction or a documented reason.

AI QA passes only when the total is at least 90/100, Accuracy and Safety are each at least 9/10, fact-checking passed, and no automatic failure remains.

## Automatic failures

- Unsupported product, prop-firm rule, regulatory, legal, fee, performance, profit, eligibility, or market claim.
- Trade signal, market forecast, personalised risk instruction, or guaranteed outcome.
- Invented source, metric, feature, firm rule, quote, testimonial, or partnership.
- Universal firm-rule wording where programs vary.
- Hidden commercial intent or product recommendation presented as neutral education.
- Broken, fabricated, misleading, or unsafe link.
- Missing material risk, limitation, assumption, or account-rule variation.
- Unresolved `Needs Fact Check`, `Prohibited`, `needs review`, or `remove` result.

## 1. Accuracy — 10

- [ ] Material claims map to current source-register IDs.
- [ ] Time-sensitive claims include checked/review dates.
- [ ] Product, feature, fee, availability, and firm-rule language is precise.
- [ ] Facts, examples, assumptions, and opinions are separated.
- [ ] No claim is stronger than its source.

## 2. Safety — 10

- [ ] Education is separate from trading or financial advice.
- [ ] No signal, forecast, profit promise, pass promise, or outcome guarantee appears.
- [ ] Risk, limitations, and firm-specific variation are visible near affected claims.
- [ ] P&L is not treated as proof of discipline or future performance.
- [ ] Examples do not become hidden risk recommendations.

## 3. Beginner Clarity — 10

- [ ] The main question is answered near the beginning.
- [ ] The article speaks directly to one trader using `you` and `your` naturally.
- [ ] A recognisable feeling or decision moment is described calmly, without shame or diagnosis.
- [ ] Technical terms are explained on first useful mention.
- [ ] Necessary trading jargon links to its approved glossary meaning when available; missing definitions are recorded as gaps.
- [ ] Sentences and paragraphs are easy to scan.
- [ ] One main idea is explained at a time.
- [ ] Very simple English and common words are used so a motivated non-trader can understand the main lesson.

## 4. Educational Value — 10

- [ ] The article explains how or why, not only what.
- [ ] Examples, steps, comparisons, or checklists make the concept practical.
- [ ] Common mistakes and trade-offs are explained.
- [ ] Process quality is separated from the trade outcome.
- [ ] The reader leaves with a safe, practical review step.

## 5. Structure — 10

- [ ] One H1 and a logical answer-first learning path are present.
- [ ] Heading levels and labels are accurate.
- [ ] Tables, lists, and callouts are used only when useful.
- [ ] Filler, repetition, abrupt transitions, and working notes are removed from publishable copy.
- [ ] Grammar, punctuation, formatting, and formulas are clean.

## 6. SEO — 10

- [ ] One canonical URL owns one clear intent.
- [ ] Title, description, slug, H1, and headings accurately satisfy that intent.
- [ ] Pillar, cluster-support, standalone, definition, or utility role is recorded.
- [ ] Cannibalisation and create/update/consolidate decisions are documented.
- [ ] GSC/search evidence is dated; unsupported demand is labelled an editorial hypothesis.

## 7. Visual Learning — 10

- [ ] The 16:9 cover follows `knowledge/06-visual-design.md` and has been checked in the existing PropLogAI desktop and mobile article layout.
- [ ] The published cover uses WebP, loads from the final article path, and has been checked for dimensions, visual quality, and file size.
- [ ] A visual, table, diagram, or comparison is used or recommended only when it improves understanding.
- [ ] Every recommendation has a teaching purpose and accessible alt-text direction.
- [ ] A handwritten chart or process is broken into progressive panels with one new point per panel and the complete view last.
- [ ] Text-heavy handwritten teaching panels use a mobile-readable ratio, normally 1:1, while the cover remains 16:9.
- [ ] Detailed teaching images support accessible click/tap zoom with a visible close control and mobile-safe enlarged view.
- [ ] Generated handwritten text, chart labels, prices, arrows, and session logic were checked for errors.
- [ ] Visuals avoid unsupported performance or safety implications.
- [ ] If no visual helps, the omission is documented and may still score 9–10.

## 8. Interactive Learning — 10

- [ ] A calculator, checklist, walkthrough, or decision aid is used or recommended only when useful.
- [ ] No interactive element was added merely to make the article look advanced.
- [ ] Assumptions and limitations are visible.
- [ ] The interaction does not provide a signal, forecast, or personalised instruction.
- [ ] The article remains understandable without the interaction.
- [ ] A well-supported omission may score 9–10.

## 9. Internal Linking — 10

- [ ] The SEO Editor selected, inserted, and checked each contextual link.
- [ ] Exact anchors describe the destination naturally.
- [ ] Blog, glossary, pillar, support, and utility relationships help the reader.
- [ ] Product links appear only when directly useful.
- [ ] Gaps or blocked links are recorded instead of forcing a link.
- [ ] The link register matches the exact revision.

## 10. PropLogAI Alignment — 10

- [ ] The complete reading experience meets the usefulness, clarity, practical teaching, glossary integration, accuracy, and safety level of the approved `/blogs/overtrading-prop-firm-challenges` benchmark without copying its structure unnecessarily.
- [ ] Tone is calm, practical, evidence-based, and non-promotional.
- [ ] The public copy teaches the trader and does not read like SEO copy, a research paper, or a sales page.
- [ ] PropLogAI is described as a journaling and discipline-review system.
- [ ] Product claims match the current approved claim register.
- [ ] The content remains useful without a product mention or CTA.
- [ ] Any CTA is optional, relevant, and separate from education.

## Required QA output

Record all ten scores with evidence, total, automatic-failure result, critical issues, minor improvements, and `PASS` or `FAIL`. `PASS` moves the exact revision to `Human Review`; it never grants publication approval.
