# Pre-Generation Checklist

- [ ] One reader, question, and search intent are defined.
- [ ] The live inventory and SEO article register were checked.
- [ ] Create, update, consolidate, redirect-proposal, or defer was decided.
- [ ] The brief is human-approved and `Ready for Production`.
- [ ] Keyword evidence and date are recorded; unsupported demand is labelled `Editorial hypothesis`.
- [ ] Content type, role, cluster, canonical URL, and pillar relationship are recorded.
- [ ] Existing contextual links and anchor patterns were checked.
- [ ] Reciprocal-link tasks and gaps are recorded.
- [ ] Every planned sensitive claim maps to a current source-register ID.
- [ ] Product/general rows are `Approved`; firm-rule rows are `Verified`.
- [ ] No required source is expired, `Needs Fact Check`, or `Prohibited`.
- [ ] Firm/program variation and formula assumptions are identified.
- [ ] Product mention and CTA are necessary or intentionally omitted.
- [ ] Output path, item ID, status, and revision are defined.
