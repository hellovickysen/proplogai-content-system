# Pre-Publish Checklist

The named human reviewer completes this checklist against the exact revision and hash after AI QA passes.

## Reader value

- [ ] The main question is answered near the top.
- [ ] A Grade 6–8 reader can understand the important ideas.
- [ ] Examples label assumptions and do not imply expected results.
- [ ] Limitations and one practical next step are visible.

## SEO and portfolio integrity

- [ ] Intent, title, description, slug, H1, and canonical owner align.
- [ ] Content type, role, cluster, pillar, and cannibalisation decision are recorded.
- [ ] SEO article register matches the exact revision.
- [ ] Internal-link register matches every contextual destination and anchor.
- [ ] Reciprocal tasks have their true status and are not falsely marked live.
- [ ] FAQ and structured-data opportunities were assessed.

## Accuracy and safety

- [ ] Current sources and source-register IDs are recorded.
- [ ] No expired, unsupported, universalized, or prohibited sensitive claim remains.
- [ ] No signal, forecast, personalised risk instruction, or outcome promise appears.
- [ ] Product capabilities and account-rule variation are precise.
- [ ] Formulas and calculations were checked independently.

## Human approval

- [ ] Fact check is `Passed`.
- [ ] AI QA is at least 90/100; Accuracy and Safety are each at least 9.
- [ ] No automatic failure remains.
- [ ] Reviewer identity, decision, timestamp, revision, and revision hash are recorded.
- [ ] Requested changes are resolved.
- [ ] Only the human reviewer sets `Approved to Publish`.
