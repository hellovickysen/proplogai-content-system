# K20 visual production notes — tracking trading emotions

- **Mode:** built-in image generation for the 16:9 cover background; deterministic SVG typography and 1:1 learning notes rendered to WebP with `worktrees/proplogai-blog/scripts/make-k20-emotion-tracking-notes.mjs`. The generated background is saved in the blog checkout as `scripts/assets/k20-emotion-tracking-cover-background.png`. No text from the image generator was relied on.
- **Cover prompt:** “Overhead handmade trading journal on a dark navy desk; simple hand-drawn XAUUSD candles approaching a level; blank paper note card; pencil; teal/violet marks; no legible words, numbers, logos, profit arrows or signals; room at top for exact title.” Exact `TRACK YOUR TRADING EMOTIONS`, `FEELING / Rushed` and `PLAN / Wait for the close` labels were composed from SVG. Final: `public/images/cover-tracking-trading-emotions-k20.webp`, 1600 × 900, 204,320 bytes.
- **Step 1:** `emotion-tracking-step-1-urge.webp` shows the before-entry feeling, urge and written candle-close check. **Step 2:** `emotion-tracking-step-2-action.webp` adds the early action and keeps the fictional +$18 net outcome separate. **Step 3:** `emotion-tracking-step-3-review.webp` shows the complete note and one question to check later. Each note is 1200 × 1200 WebP. The labels are fixed in source, not generated as image text.
- **Visual QA:** Inspected all four saved images. The first cover composite had a plan label too low; adjusted and regenerated it. The final cover label fits inside the note card. The three notes have no cropped text. The article uses the existing accessible `ZoomImage` component. At 390 px, the page had no horizontal overflow and the first note's modal opened and closed.
