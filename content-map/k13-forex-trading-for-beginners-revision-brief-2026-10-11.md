## What the existing article needs fixed

1. Replace the long list of abstract “foundational concepts” with a clear learning sequence and one short worked decision. The present page is text-heavy and does not show a beginner how the ideas connect.
2. Remove unsupported claims that most beginners fail for a particular reason, one gap is the biggest, one hour of study could have prevented failure, risk management ensures account survival, or the guide removes common causes of failure. Avoid implying journaling or a plan guarantees a pass.
3. Explain **EURUSD as a currency pair**. If the example uses **XAUUSD**, say it is gold quoted in US dollars, not a currency pair. Do not teach a generic pip/lot formula that would be wrong for a different instrument or contract. Explain leverage as greater exposure that can magnify losses as well as gains.
4. Replace unlinked quoted “companion article” titles with actual relevant destinations. Connect the first useful uses of terms to glossary definitions, not a dense link list. If there is no suitable glossary entry, define the term in plain English on the page.
5. Describe a prop-firm challenge as **one specific firm's program with its own current terms**. Profit targets, loss limits, trading-day requirements, measurement and breach consequences vary. Do not imply every challenge has every listed condition.
6. Narrow the PropLogAI paragraph to manual journaling and supported views from recorded data (PLAI-002–005). No automatic broker sync, rule compliance proof or outcome promise.

## Teaching flow for local article revision 1

1. Open with a direct moment: “You see an XAUUSD London-session breakout and feel ready to trade. Before you click, do you know what you are trading, what could make the trade lose money, and what your account rules allow?” State that this is a fictional learning scene, not an entry suggestion.
2. First basics: a forex pair such as EURUSD compares two currencies; XAUUSD is gold quoted in USD. A price move matters only together with **how much you trade**. Explain trade size, spread/cost, and leverage in one or two plain sentences each, with links only where the portfolio has a good definition.
3. Show one compact fictional **“before the trade” note** for an XAUUSD breakout: planned setup and session; intended stop-loss; a hypothetical **$50 planned loss**; whether the exact firm rule and daily room have been checked. Do not invent a lot size, live price, guaranteed maximum loss or firm limit. Explicitly say actual loss may differ from a plan.
4. Move from the example to a simple learning order: learn instrument and order basics → practise a setup and plan on demo → calculate size and planned risk → read the exact firm's current rules → journal and review your own trades → use the separate readiness checklist before paying. These are learning tasks, not a fixed timeline or a claim of readiness.
5. Give one short “what would you do?” moment: if you cannot explain your stop, estimated USD risk or firm's loss formula yet, keep learning and testing the plan. Do not prescribe a trade or personal risk percentage.
6. Finish with three useful next links, chosen by the reader's question: the [position-size guide](/blogs/how-to-calculate-position-size-forex), [prop-firm rules guide](/blogs/prop-firm-rules-guide), and [challenge-readiness checklist](/blogs/prop-firm-challenge-readiness). A short, factual PropLogAI note may explain manual logging and later review.
