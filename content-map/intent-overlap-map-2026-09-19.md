## Cross-link priorities

- Risk-management blogs -> drawdown, daily drawdown, risk per trade, position sizing, stop loss, and risk-reward glossary pages.
- Psychology blogs -> FOMO, revenge trading, tilt, confirmation bias, loss aversion, and overconfidence terms.
- Journal blogs -> trading journal, trade review, emotion tracking, pattern recognition, performance report, and AI trading coach terms.
- Discipline blogs -> trading plan, setup compliance, overtrading, pre-market routine, trade management, and rule-based trading terms.
- Prop-firm blogs -> prop-firm challenge, funded account, overall drawdown, profit target, and consistency rule terms.
- Each glossary page -> one relevant deeper blog guide after that guide passes its source and quality review.
