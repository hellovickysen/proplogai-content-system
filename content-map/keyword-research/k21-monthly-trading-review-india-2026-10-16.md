## Live page and intent ownership

- Opened the [live article](https://proplogai.com/blogs/monthly-trading-review-template) on 16 October 2026. It has one H1, a 18 July 2026 publication date, a five-part prompt list, no filled month, no contextual glossary links, and repeated assertions that several weeks reveal a stable pattern. It also claims an in-app month-by-month lookup of win rate, profit factor and compliance, which needs a current product-claim check before reuse. The article calls a weekly `70%, 65%, 68%` sequence a story despite the missing trade counts and denominator.
- Untouched local source: `worktrees/proplogai-blog/src/content/blog/monthly-trading-review-template.md`, SHA-256 `467EDF5615E0C8A5C12F436FA901063E9FC0F6E29BF09F64F4FAF8A14AF569D2`.
- The [journal pillar](https://proplogai.com/blogs/prop-firm-trading-journal) owns the whole record-and-review workflow. The [weekly template](https://proplogai.com/blogs/weekly-trading-review-template) owns one week and one next-week check. The [performance metrics guide](https://proplogai.com/blogs/trading-performance-metrics) explains metrics, while the [P&L calendar guide](https://proplogai.com/blogs/prop-firm-pnl-calendar) owns calendar-based review. K21 should own **comparing several weekly notes in one month, keeping counts and dollar results separate, checking a repeated observation against the underlying trades, and choosing one next-month question**.
- The [trade-review glossary](https://proplogai.com/glossary/trade-review) owns the short definition; the [performance-report glossary](https://proplogai.com/glossary/performance-report) owns the report meaning. The article can link them when the term first helps the reader, without duplicating those definitions.

## Decision

Keep `/blogs/monthly-trading-review-template`, its publication date, Trading Journaling category and journal-cluster role. The title/keyword remain intent-led hypotheses because current India search metrics for the exact phrase are unavailable. Rewrite locally as a genuinely copyable monthly worksheet plus a completed fictional month, with numbers that reconcile and explicit `not recorded` / `not enough comparable records` paths. Do not imply that a few weeks prove a persistent behaviour, a rule breach, or future trading results. Set an updated date only when the article changes.
