## Current result pattern

Current results are dominated by interactive calculators that request win rate or win/loss counts plus average win and average loss. Common supporting sections explain the formula, break-even logic, costs, sample size and the difference between expectancy and profit factor.

Several results overstate what positive expectancy proves or project future totals as if they were reliable. PropLogAI should avoid that framing. The calculator must describe the selected historical sample or user-entered scenario and must not predict the next trade or guarantee future profit.

## Decision

Proceed with a simple calculator-led utility page at `/blogs/trading-expectancy-calculator`. Use trade counts plus average net win and average net loss so the tool can derive the rates and cross-check the sample result. Keep the definition at `/glossary/expectancy` and the combined interpretation workflow at `/blogs/trading-performance-metrics`.
