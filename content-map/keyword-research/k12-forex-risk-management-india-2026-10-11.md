## Claim checks

- The live article says a stop-loss turns planned risk into **“I can't lose more than this”**, and ends by claiming risk management **“keeps any single bad trade, bad day, or bad stretch from being the one that ends the account.”** Both overstate control. [FTMO's order-type explanation](https://ftmo.com/en/blog/order-types/) and [slippage explanation](https://ftmo.com/en/blog/slippage-order-execution/) say a stop-loss may fill away from the requested price. A planned loss is an estimate, not a maximum fill guarantee. These sources were checked on 11 October 2026; they describe FTMO's execution and illustrate the general limitation, not every firm's contract.
- The article treats daily and overall prop-firm limits as uniformly hard. [FTMO Trading Objectives](https://ftmo.com/en/trading-objectives/) show that loss calculations differ between its 1-Step and 2-Step programs and include open positions, swaps, and commissions in equity. [FTMO Futures' own FAQ](https://ftmo.com/en/futures/faq/what-are-the-trading-objectives-and-what-happens-if-i-violate-them/) even distinguishes soft and hard limits. The revised article must tell the reader to check the exact firm, program, stage, formula, reset time and consequence rather than treating all limits alike. Do not transplant FTMO percentages into generic teaching.
- “The gap between entry and stop multiplied by position size produces a dollar figure” is an oversimplified instrument formula. For XAUUSD, the value depends on the contract/units and account currency; spread, commission, swap and execution can change actual results. The revised article should teach inputs and refer exact size calculation to the existing guide/tool without giving a universal lot formula.
- The product paragraph implies planned dollar risk, actual stop placement, and daily totals can be logged and used to verify compliance. The approved PropLogAI feature register **PLAI-002–005** supports manual trade details, notes, emotions, rule adherence, screenshots, dashboard measures and a P&L calendar. It does not establish an immutable original-stop trail, broker-verified actual risk, or automatic compliance proof. Narrow the product copy accordingly.
- The article names “Support and Resistance for Beginner Forex Traders” and “How to Choose the Best Forex Pairs for Your Trading Style” as related reads without linking verified current portfolio URLs. Remove or replace with quality-checked pages only where they help the reader.
