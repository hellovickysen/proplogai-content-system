## Factual checks that shape the revision

- The existing opening treats daily limits, consistency, trading-day requirements and payout checks as one standard rule set. [FTMO's current official objectives](https://ftmo.com/en/trading-objectives/) distinguish programs and stages and include date-dependent legacy/current terms. Checked 12 October 2026. This is evidence that terms vary, **not** a rule to apply to every firm. The article should tell a reader to check their own current agreement and dashboard, including account creation/transition date where relevant.
- The page's “Did I stay inside the funded account rules?” and “whether the trade was allowed” wording suggests a self-entered journal can verify compliance. It cannot establish an official breach, payout eligibility or remaining buffer; formulas may use equity, open P&L, costs and firm-specific reset times. Record a check and its source, then verify status in the firm's official account view.
- The five-point discipline score, suggested two-setup stopping rule and automatic size reduction are author's methods, not universal firm rules or verified PropLogAI features. Either remove them or clearly make a trader's *own optional written-plan check* the subject. Do not let profit/loss alone score discipline.
- In the existing R explanation, actual loss is not always exactly planned risk after execution and costs. State the denominator and whether the example uses net P&L. If no calculation teaches the funded-account task, link the pillar and avoid repeating it.
- The current product claim register PLAI-002–005 (review due 19–24 October 2026) supports manual trade records, notes/emotions/rule adherence, screenshots, dashboard metrics and logged-data P&L calendar. It does not substantiate an automatic firm-rule validator, automatic size reduction, a built-in five-point score or a built-in weekly-summary generator. Recheck first-party product pages before using a feature claim in revised public copy.
