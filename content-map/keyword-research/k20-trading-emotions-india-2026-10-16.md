## Current page and portfolio ownership

- Opened the [live article](https://proplogai.com/blogs/tracking-trading-emotions) on 16 October 2026. It returned the expected page with one H1 and a 7 July 2026 publication date. It has broad, repeated diary-versus-log sections and no contextual glossary links in the body. It asserts that a pre-entry tag captures what influenced a decision, suggests a fixed five-to-eight-tag range, uses an unsupported 15% illustration, and describes specific PropLogAI emotion-tag/Coach behaviour that needs first-party verification.

K20 should own **a simple, repeatable way to record feeling, urge, planned check and action at the same point in each trade, then review several entries without treating emotion as a cause or a diagnosis**.

## Decision

Keep `/blogs/tracking-trading-emotions`, its 7 July 2026 publication date and cluster-support role. A shorter, task-led H1 and meta title are worth testing in the local revision, but the current evidence does not justify moving the URL. Set an honest updated date only after implementation. Do not imply emotion predicts a result, prescribe a universal tag count, equate a trader's note with a firm verdict, or claim app features without checking them.
