# Keyword Research Evidence

The active primary market is India in English. Use dated India evidence files in this folder for the keyword or cluster being reviewed. Older seed files are a baseline, not permanent current data.

Semrush is accessed directly through the signed-in NoxTools browser page. Use Server 1 first, switch to Servers 2–6 when login, limit, or data failures require it, and save the server, India database, exact query, checked date, reported metrics, and unavailable fields. Notify the user when all usable servers fail. Do not guess unavailable values or submit a support ticket without explicit instruction.

Search Console remains the source of truth for PropLogAI's own search performance. Semrush and Ubersuggest provide market estimates and discovery evidence; keep their figures separate from GSC clicks, impressions, CTR, and positions.

`ubersuggest-seed-metrics-2026-09-24.csv` is the earlier United States research. It is retained as historical secondary-market evidence and must not drive India priorities or be copied into active India metrics.

Other-country research is allowed only under `docs/audience-and-market-policy.md`. Keep each market separate and dated.
