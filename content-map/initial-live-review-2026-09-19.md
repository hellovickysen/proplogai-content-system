# Initial PropLogAI Blog and Glossary Review

## Scope

This is a first-pass collection-page review of `https://proplogai.com/blogs` and `https://proplogai.com/glossary`, checked on 2026-09-19. It establishes the starting point for a full URL-level audit; it is not a complete technical or article-by-article audit.

## Inventory snapshot

- Blog index: 24 articles across AI Trading Coaching, General Forex Education, Prop Firm Expenses and ROI, Tools and Calculators, Trading Discipline, Trading Journaling, and Trading Psychology.
- Glossary index: 36 terms across Trading Psychology, Risk Management, Performance Metrics, Trading Discipline, Prop Firm, and Journal & Analysis.

## Priority findings

### P1: brand typo on blog collection page

The CTA reads `personalized coaching from Propol AI`. Confirm the source component and correct the product name to `PropLogAI`.

### P1: glossary numeric claims need source-by-source review

Several summaries use broad ranges or benchmarks for daily drawdown, overall drawdown, risk per trade, profit factor, profit targets, and profit splits. Prop-firm rules differ by firm, program, account type, and date. Name the rule owner and checked date or replace the number with a clear variation note.

### P1: funded-account wording needs precision

The current summary says a funded account is provided with a firm's capital. Some programs use simulated accounts or different contractual models. The definition should avoid a universal capital claim and tell the reader to check the named firm's current terms.

### P2: older blog language conflicts with the current brand standard

Collection cards include phrases such as `account killer`, `making you lose money`, and `single biggest edge`. Audit these older articles for outcome claims, fear-based framing, current sources, and advice boundaries before using them as internal-link targets.

### P2: content architecture is promising but needs canonical mapping

The live categories align with PropLogAI's product scope. The next audit should map all 24 articles and 36 terms by intent, identify overlapping definitions and guides, and build two-way blog-to-glossary links.

## Next audit pass

Capture every canonical URL, HTTP result, metadata, H1, sitemap presence, schema, last-updated signal, source register, internal links, and content score. Do not plan new content until overlapping intent is checked against that inventory.
