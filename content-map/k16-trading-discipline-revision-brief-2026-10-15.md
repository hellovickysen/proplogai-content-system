## Fixes to the existing copy

1. Start at a concrete decision: XAUUSD moves beyond a level marked before the London session. Your breakout plan requires a candle close, but you feel an urge to enter early. Define XAUUSD and breakout in a sentence. Ask what your own plan required, what you did, and what you recorded.
2. Replace the broad willpower argument and the six repetitive steps with a simple daily loop: before the session, at the decision, after the trade, and during the later review. Do not reproduce the complete checklist, rulebook template, or weekly-review worksheet. Link those specialist pages where useful.
3. Remove the fixed “pause after two losses” and fixed cooling-off examples as implied best practice. A stop or pause condition must come from the trader's own written plan or the current official terms of their exact firm, program, stage and account. Do not assign a universal risk amount, trade count, session limit or breach outcome.
4. Keep **plan followed?** separate from **net P&L**. A +$ result after skipping a required check is still a plan change worth reviewing; a losing trade that followed the plan is not automatically undisciplined. Use a short fictional USD comparison, with no live XAUUSD entry, exit, target or price prediction.
5. Narrow PropLogAI copy to approved manual journal, emotion and rule-adherence records (PLAI-001/002/005). Do not imply it reads the live broker, blocks a trade, verifies an official firm rule, diagnoses a psychological condition, or promises better performance. Recheck the first-party product page when writing.
6. Explain any necessary jargon on first use and link a relevant, verified glossary definition. Use the reader's words wherever a plain sentence is clearer than a trading label.

## Proposed teaching flow

1. **Quick answer:** discipline means comparing the current decision with conditions you wrote before pressure appeared, then reviewing what happened. It is not measured by a single win or loss.
2. **Before the session:** one fictional XAUUSD London breakout plan with setup, candle-close confirmation, size method and personal stop condition. Keep firm-specific numbers out of the example; instruct the reader to check their exact official account rules.
3. **At the decision:** show the fork: the confirmation is complete or it is still missing. An urge is information to note, not proof of failure. A missing required check means this action does not match the written plan.
4. **After the trade:** record plan check and USD net result on separate lines, then review a few sessions for repeated changes. One example does not prove a pattern or cause.
5. **Next session:** choose one personal check to make easier to use. Route the reader to the complete checklist, rulebook, journal and psychology guides instead of duplicating them.
