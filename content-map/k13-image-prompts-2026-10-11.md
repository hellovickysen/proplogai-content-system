## Cover — `cover-forex-trading-for-beginners-k13.webp`

> Create a polished 16:9 handmade notebook-style educational blog cover, cream paper with dark navy handwriting and cyan/yellow marker strokes. Big exact text 'FOREX, ONE STEP AT A TIME' and small exact text 'Before the challenge'. Simple pen sketches of EURUSD as two currency symbols, XAUUSD as a gold bar labelled 'Gold in USD', and a blank journal. Keep visual sparse and mobile-thumbnail readable. No prices, trading signal arrows, profit claims, logos, tiny text, or AI-glossy effects. Opaque background.
