## Decision

PropLogAI primarily serves Indian users. Active content planning, keyword metrics, GSC decisions, examples, and review defaults now use India in English. Other countries may receive content when their own GSC, keyword, customer, or product evidence proves a distinct opportunity. Country metrics cannot be blended.
