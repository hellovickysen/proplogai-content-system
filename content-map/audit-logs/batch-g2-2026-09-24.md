3. The queue and SEO register initially showed a pre-draft state. The idempotent pilot registration and hash scripts now align them to the current `Human Review` revision.
4. PowerShell CSV export quoted the queue header, while the system validator expected unquoted text. Header validation now accepts standard CSV quoting and still enforces the exact queue contract.

No validator false positive remains known after these fixes. Human review time and revision count remain pending until the reviewer accepts the draft or requests changes.
