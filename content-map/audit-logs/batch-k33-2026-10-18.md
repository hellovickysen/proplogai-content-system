## Important live finding

Browser checks of `https://proplogai.com/blogs/trading-journal-benefits` and `https://proplogai.com/blogs/trading-emotions-account-killer` both ended on a **404** after redirecting to a path without `/blogs`. The standalone blog project's `vercel.json` used path-relative destinations. K33 changes both destinations to absolute `https://proplogai.com/blogs/...` URLs and extends the build validator to require them. **The production redirects remain broken until this local correction is released and verified.** The main-site repository was not edited.

The SEO register now marks the retired journal URL `redirect_error` rather than live, assigns the intended journal pillar as its canonical owner, and records the observed failure. Both migration-register rows now say that a local repair is prepared and the live redirect is broken; their earlier approval dates remain historical. The category model now lists `/blogs/prop-firm-rules-guide` as the current Prop Firm Rules pillar and challenge readiness as support.
