- Completed: 2026-09-24
- Scope: local PropLogAI content system
- Market for live keyword checks: United States / English
- GSC evidence period: 2026-07-06 through 2026-09-17
- Production effect: none

## Objective

Turn the audited blog and glossary inventory into an operational SEO portfolio: stable categories, topic clusters, canonical ownership, supporting keywords, pillar relationships, cross-type links, measured opportunities, and a prioritised future-content backlog.

## Work completed

1. Defined eight subject categories plus navigation and separated subject taxonomy from formats such as calculators and templates.
2. Assigned all 61 inventory URLs a category, topic cluster, content role, primary keyword, supporting keywords, search intent, pillar URL, pillar status, paired blog/glossary URLs, evidence type, action, priority, and review date.
3. Preserved the dated GSC performance fields and canonical ownership decisions.
4. Captured 16 live Ubersuggest seed metrics for the United States in English. Seven existing portfolio rows use an exact measured primary keyword; unmeasured phrases remain blank and are never treated as zero.
5. Created a 16-row opportunity backlog: eight P1, five P2, and three P3 items spanning updates, new pages, one consolidation proposal, one engineering-dependent utility, and one deferred product/canonical decision.
6. Expanded the durable internal-link register from 24 to 152 decisions. The 22 locally verified and two live-verified statuses were preserved; 128 new relationships are `planned` until their exact source revisions are edited and reviewed.
7. Made the SEO portfolio builder preserve curated mapping fields during GSC refreshes.
8. Expanded deterministic validation to require complete portfolio fields, valid evidence labels, valid pillar ownership, known paired URLs, and non-invented numeric metrics.
9. Created a proposed twelve-slot Q4 sequence that starts with one low-risk pilot and keeps source-sensitive or dependency-blocked work behind explicit gates.
