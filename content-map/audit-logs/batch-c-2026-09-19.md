## Expiry and failure behavior

`knowledge/11-source-expiry-rules.md` defines maximum review windows and earlier recheck triggers:

- 7 days for pricing, promotions, trials, plan limits, and coming-soon features.
- 30 days for other PropLogAI capabilities.
- 14 days for named prop-firm rules, laws, regulations, tax, and platform policies.
- 12 months for stable peer-reviewed research.
- 30 days for benchmarks, surveys, market statistics, and performance claims.

An expired source automatically becomes `Needs Fact Check` for editorial purposes. Missing, expired, `Needs Fact Check`, or `Prohibited` claims fail closed at pre-generation, editorial QA, and automation approval.
