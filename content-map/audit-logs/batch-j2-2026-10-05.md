# Batch J2 Audit Log — Risk-Reward Ratio and Stop Loss Briefs

## Scope

Prepared revision 1 briefs for two P2 glossary corrections. No glossary implementation, push, deployment or production change was made.

## Audit findings

- `/glossary/risk-reward-ratio` presents simplified breakeven arithmetic as a profitability rule, says higher ratios create more room for error, and recommends a universal `1:1.5 to 1:3` sweet spot with a win rate above 40%.
- The same page says PropLogAI automatically calculates planned R:R and compares it with achieved R:R. Those product claims are not approved by the product source register.
- `/glossary/stop-loss` says a stop automatically caps the loss and calls stops the primary defence against catastrophic loss and universal survival tools.
- The stop-loss page does not distinguish the planned stop level, trigger and actual fill, and it does not explain that platform terminology and order mechanics vary.
- The stop-loss product connection claims automatic stop recording, live behaviour identification and AI warnings that are not approved by the product source register.
- The related risk-management blog owns the wider workflow. J2 will keep the glossary pages focused on definition and execution limits rather than duplicating that guide.

## Evidence decisions

- Added `RES-014` from CME Group for the limited claim that risk-reward ratio can be one input in a trade plan. The source's percentage example is not a universal recommendation.
- Added `RES-015` from FINRA for the trigger-versus-fill and stop-limit non-execution concepts.
- Recorded that `RES-015` describes US stock orders. Exact forex, CFD, futures, broker, platform and prop-firm mechanics require their own specifications.
- Limited product wording to approved manual trade logging, rule-adherence records, and the dashboard's average-R display.
- Current India GSC and Semrush metrics remain pending. The authenticated Chrome tabs were not exposed to the browser-control session on 5 October 2026, so no values were inferred or backfilled.

## Revision 1 brief decisions

- Keep both existing URLs; no migration is justified.
- Risk-Reward Ratio will use a fictional XAUUSD London-session example with `$50` planned risk and `$100` planned reward, then separate the planned ratio from realised R.
- Simplified breakeven win rates may be shown only as arithmetic before costs and execution differences, with an immediate warning that they do not predict profitability.
- Stop Loss will explain the path from planned level to trigger to attempted execution and actual fill, including slippage and non-execution risk.
- Each page may receive one square handwritten WebP learning note with click/tap zoom if the implemented visual remains clearer than text alone.
- Both pages will link to their related definitions and the risk-management pillar without absorbing calculator, expectancy or full-workflow intent.
