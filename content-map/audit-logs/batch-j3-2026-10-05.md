# Batch J3 Audit Log — Win Rate and Profit Factor

## Scope

Prepared, implemented and approved revision 1 for two P1 performance-metric glossary refreshes. All work remains local. No push, deployment or production change was made.

## Audit findings

- `/glossary/win-rate` has a correct basic formula but uses a separate 100-trade example rather than the approved 20-trade XAUUSD sample in the performance-metrics pillar.
- The win-rate page needs a clearer explanation of breakeven trades, partial exits, multi-leg positions, costs, outcome size and rule adherence.
- `/glossary/profit-factor` has a correct formula and already avoids a universal benchmark, but its example does not align with the pillar's sample.
- The profit-factor product connection claims monthly breakdowns and says the metric shows how an edge evolves. Those statements exceed the approved dashboard claim.
- The page does not explain what happens when gross loss is zero or why platforms may display a special result.
- Both pages use only the PropLogAI product source. J3 adds independent technical documentation for the metric inputs and formula.

## Evidence decisions

- Added `RES-016` from the official MQL5 testing-statistics documentation for total trades, profitable trades, gross profit, gross loss and profit factor.
- Restricted the source to its technical definitions. A Strategy Tester statistic does not prove future performance or universal reporting conventions.
- Reused the approved 20-trade XAUUSD sample from `/blogs/trading-performance-metrics`: 8 wins averaging $180 and 12 losses averaging $80.
- Semrush India Server 1 evidence from 3 October 2026 reports `profit factor` volume 140, KD 11, informational intent and CPC $0. A new exact browser refresh was unavailable on 5 October because authenticated Chrome was not exposed.
- The GSC family check for 6 July–29 September 2026 returned 0 clicks and 0 impressions for performance-metric queries.
- No exact India Semrush metric is recorded for `trading win rate`; it remains an editorial hypothesis.

## Revision 1 brief decisions

- Keep both existing URLs; no migration is justified.
- Use the same fictional XAUUSD sample as the live performance-metrics pillar so all formulas reconcile.
- Win Rate will teach counting rules and the limits of frequency alone.
- Profit Factor will teach gross winning amount versus absolute gross losing amount, the zero-loss edge case and sample sensitivity.
- Each page may receive one square handwritten WebP note with click/tap zoom after brief approval.
- Product wording is limited to dashboard metrics calculated from logged data.
