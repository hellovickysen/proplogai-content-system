## Current content risks

### Setup Compliance

- Uses invented 58% versus 35% win rates.
- Claims those numbers diagnose that a strategy works while discipline does not.
- Treats every criterion as universally binary.
- Claims PropLogAI automatically compares compliant and non-compliant performance.

### Trade Management

- Says active management is more profitable in skilled hands.
- Recommends a universal partial-exit and breakeven-stop sequence.
- Claims watching a trade makes poor decisions more likely.
- Cites unnamed prop-firm studies saying set-and-forget outperforms.
- Overstates AI-coach identification of management mistakes.

## Revision 1 brief decisions

- Setup Compliance will become a neutral comparison between prewritten conditions and the recorded action using `Met`, `Not met` and `Not recorded`.
- Trade Management will define after-entry choices without recommending one method.
- Both pages will reuse the J10 fictional XAUUSD London-session breakout-and-retest context.
- Both pages will separate P&L from whether the recorded action matched the rule.
- Both pages will remove unsupported performance, behavioural and product claims.
- Each brief plans one square handwritten WebP with click/tap zoom after approval.
- Any table introduced during implementation must scroll horizontally inside its own container and must not create page-level overflow.
