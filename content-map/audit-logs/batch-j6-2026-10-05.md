### Average Win vs Average Loss

- Separated the average-win and average-loss formulas.
- Reused the approved fictional 20-trade XAUUSD sample: 8 winners produced $1,440 and 12 losers lost $960.
- Calculated a $180 average win, an $80 average loss and a 2.25:1 realised outcome-size ratio.
- Distinguished the realised ratio from a planned risk-reward ratio.
- Explained why the two averages must be read with win rate and expectancy.
- Added five sample-definition checks covering the date range, counting rules, units, costs and unusual outliers.
- Added verified links to Win Rate, Expectancy, Profit Factor, Risk-Reward Ratio, the Trading Expectancy Calculator and the Trading Performance Metrics guide.

### Sharpe Ratio

- Rewrote the definition in plain English and explained standard deviation without assuming prior knowledge.
- Added a fictional monthly example: (2% − 0.5%) ÷ 3% = 0.50.
- Added five comparison checks covering the return series, frequency, baseline, period and method.
- Separated Sharpe ratio from a prop-firm best-day or best-trade consistency rule.
- Stated the limits clearly: it does not show the equity-curve shape, drawdown timing, tail risk, trade count or future performance by itself.
- Added verified links to Consistency Rule, Equity Curve, Drawdown, Win Rate, Average Win vs Average Loss, Expectancy and the Trading Performance Metrics guide.
