Speak to this trader directly in very simple English. India / English is the primary audience; forex amounts stay in USD (`$`).

## What the revision must teach

1. Open with the trader's missing-information problem: “I made $18, but did I follow my breakout plan?” The fictional XAUUSD London example should name the setup and explain it in one plain sentence. Do not state a live price, entry, target or timing signal.
2. Give a **small starting set** in the order a trader can actually fill it: (a) date, symbol and session; (b) setup and the written check; (c) planned stop/size or risk method; (d) what the trader actually did and whether the planned check happened; (e) net P&L in USD; (f) one short review note. Explain each field through the one running example rather than a new list for each field. These are suggested review fields, not universal requirements.
3. Show a compact filled **before / during / after** journal example. Include one **$ result** and a separate **plan-followed?** answer. A profit does not prove the trader followed the plan; a loss does not prove they broke it. Any arithmetic must be deterministic and checkable.
4. Add optional fields only when they answer a real question: brief emotion tag, annotated screenshot, reason for a changed stop or exit, and a record of which official firm rule or account condition the trader checked. Do not imply PropLogAI automatically verifies a prop-firm account or computes a breach verdict. The trader's note is not the firm's official status.
5. Replace the present 11 sections and repetitive nested lists with one easy teaching flow. Explain “setup,” “stop,” “net P&L,” and any necessary jargon beside first use, linking reviewed glossary definitions where they exist. Remove R-multiple if it adds complexity; if retained, explain it accurately and link its definition.
6. End by helping the trader remove a field that never informs a review question and add one missing field after repeated confusion. The distinct [copyable trading journal template](/blogs/trading-journal-template) is the next step for a reusable form; do not duplicate its CSV or turn K19 into a second template.
7. Recheck every PropLogAI product assertion against the current approved claim register and first-party UI. Keep the product mention brief and limited to what is verified about manual records/review. Recheck any account-rule statement against current official terms or remove it. No signals, outcome promises, or universal firm limits.
