## Correct the old article

1. Open at a trader's question, not a trend claim. Example: after recording a week of fictional XAUUSD London-session trades, you ask, **“Which entries did I mark as entering before my written candle-close check?”** Explain XAUUSD and the example setup in one plain sentence. Do not give a live price, direction, entry, target or session-timing signal.
2. Replace the repeated “should/should never” and misconceptions lists with one short practical flow: **ask → inspect source entries → correct a wrong/missing label → draw a limited conclusion**. Do not recreate K9's detailed eight-trade counting exercise or the glossary's definition. The pillar should help the reader decide whether a coach answer is worth trusting.
3. Make the answer visibly conditional on manual records. A model can summarise what a trader entered but cannot recover an unrecorded trade, know an unlogged feeling, verify a broker statement, prove why a pattern occurred, or enforce a firm's actual account terms. One convincing-sounding answer is not proof.
4. Remove or qualify unverified claims: automatic emotion-trigger detection, session-specific weakness detection, reliable correlation discovery, a guaranteed faster review, and “PropLogAI Coach analyses P&L history” as though every calculation has been verified. Separate **what an AI journal coach could help review** from **what PropLogAI currently supports** under PLAI-001/002/005. The live homepage's unsupported statistics, prescribed cooldown example and inconsistent plan limits are not evidence for this article.
5. Explain the difference between a review question about **past recorded decisions** and a signal about **the next trade** without using legal conclusions such as “unlicensed advice.” Avoid promises of passing a challenge, finding an edge, improving a win rate, or preventing a breach. Firm rules vary by exact program, stage and account date; this article is not a firm-rule checker.
6. Include a few questions a reader can use: What records and date range did the answer use? Which entries support it? Are labels and results correct? What is missing? Did the AI turn an observation into a prediction? Keep the final action with the trader.
