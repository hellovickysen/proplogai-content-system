# Source Registers

These registers are the claim gate for PropLogAI blog and glossary work.

- `proplogai-feature-claims.csv` controls product, plan, price, limit, integration, and marketing claims.
- `prop-firm-rules.csv` controls named firm and program rules.
- `general-research.csv` controls behavioral, statistical, benchmark, and market claims.

Only `Approved` product/research rows and `Verified` firm-rule rows may be stated as facts. A `Needs Fact Check` row blocks drafting or publication of that claim. A `Prohibited` row records wording that must not be reused.

Every use must remain within the wording and limitations recorded in the row. Editors must recheck a source when `review_by` has passed or when a product, program, price, policy, or research interpretation may have changed.
