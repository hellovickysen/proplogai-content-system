# PropLogAI Topic Clusters

This map begins with the live collection categories observed on 2026-09-19. Treat priorities as editorial hypotheses until current Search Console evidence is available.

## 1. Trading journaling and reviews

Pillar opportunity: practical trading journal system for prop-firm traders.

Support: journal fields, daily review, weekly review, monthly review, emotion tags, screenshots, setup compliance, and review templates.

## 2. Trading discipline and rulebooks

Pillar opportunity: practical discipline system for prop-firm traders.

Support: written rulebooks, breach tracking, overtrading, post-loss routines, session limits, pre-trade checks, and one-focus practice.

## 3. Trading psychology

Pillar opportunity: how behaviour causes rule drift in prop-firm trading.

Support: revenge trading, FOMO, tilt, overconfidence, loss aversion, confirmation bias, and structured emotion tracking.

## 4. Prop-firm rules and readiness

Pillar opportunity: how to read and compare prop-firm rules without assuming they are universal.

Support: challenge readiness, daily loss, overall drawdown, consistency rules, payout conditions, funded-account models, and firm-rule change checks.

## 5. Risk and performance review

Pillar opportunity: reviewing risk and performance metrics without treating outcomes as discipline scores.

Support: position sizing, risk-reward, expectancy, profit factor, win rate, average win versus loss, equity curves, and drawdown.

## 6. Expenses, payouts, and ROI

Pillar opportunity: measuring the full cost of prop-firm participation.

Support: challenge fees, resets, activations, payouts, failed attempts, account-level costs, and ROI formulas with assumptions.

## 7. AI coaching, tools, and calculators

Pillar opportunity: responsible AI review of a trader's own journal data.

Support: pattern detection, coaching boundaries, drawdown calculators, consistency calculators, templates, and data-quality limits.

## Cross-type rule

Glossary pages own definitions. Blog pages own workflows, comparisons, templates, and deeper explanations. Each cluster needs a canonical intent map before new pages are approved.
