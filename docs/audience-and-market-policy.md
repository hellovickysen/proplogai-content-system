# PropLogAI Audience and Market Policy

## Primary audience

PropLogAI primarily serves traders in India. The default SEO market is `India / English`, and the default reader is an Indian prop-firm trader using English.

Use clear Indian English and IST when time or session context matters. Forex trading values, account figures, risk, costs, and P&L examples use USD with the `$` symbol. India defines the primary audience; it does not make forex examples INR-denominated. Do not force a geographic modifier into a title, heading, keyword, or URL when Indian searchers use the unmodified term.

## Primary-market evidence

- Run keyword tools with India as the location and English as the language.
- Filter Search Console by country `ind` for active India decisions.
- Store the country, language, source, and checked date beside every metric.
- Keep global and other-country exports as dated historical or secondary evidence. Never blend volumes from multiple countries into one metric.
- Blank means unmeasured. Zero is valid only when a named source returned zero for the exact market and date.

## Other-country opportunities

Content for another country is allowed when current country-specific GSC, keyword, customer, or product evidence shows a meaningful opportunity.

Every secondary-country proposal must record:

1. target country and language;
2. query and intent difference from the India owner;
3. product availability and claim differences;
4. whether the India page can satisfy the intent;
5. the canonical, localisation, and hreflang decision; and
6. a separate performance measurement plan.

Do not create country pages by swapping country names. A separate URL requires a materially different reader task or country-specific facts. Otherwise, keep one India-first canonical page that remains understandable internationally.

## India-specific claim controls

Regulatory, tax, payment, foreign-exchange, platform-access, and legal claims require current first-party Indian sources and an explicit checked date. Use the relevant official regulator, government department, payment provider, or product owner. Do not infer legality, eligibility, tax treatment, or availability from search results or competitor copy.

## Content defaults

- Explain global prop-firm terms without assuming a US tax system or timezone.
- Use USD and `$` consistently for forex and prop-firm trading amounts. Do not introduce INR merely because the reader is in India.
- Use IST only when the reader needs a local time reference.
- Treat named-firm rules as variable and verify the exact program.
- Preserve the no-signals, no-profit-promises, and no-personalised-advice boundaries.
