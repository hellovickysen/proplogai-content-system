# PropLogAI Category and Topical-Cluster Model

- Effective date: 2026-09-24
- Primary market for live keyword checks: India / English
- Secondary markets: allowed only with separate country-specific evidence under `docs/audience-and-market-policy.md`
- Portfolio scope: blogs and glossary pages

Categories are reader-facing shelves. Topic clusters define search ownership and internal-link relationships. A calculator or template is a content role inside a subject cluster, not a separate topic category.

## Ownership rules

- One primary intent has one canonical owner.
- Glossary pages own concise definition intent. Blogs own workflows, comparisons, templates, checklists, and deeper explanations.
- Every non-pillar page links to its current pillar when useful. Planned pillars are recorded but are not linked until live and quality checked.
- Blog-to-glossary links explain unfamiliar terms. Glossary-to-blog links lead to practical application.
- Categories do not change merely to chase a keyword. A page moves only when its reader job and canonical ownership change.
- Firm-rule content must name the firm/program when a rule is specific and retain checked dates and variation limits.
