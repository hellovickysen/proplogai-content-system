# Blog and Glossary Roles

## Blog

A blog article answers a practical question in depth. It may teach a process, compare approaches, provide a template, explain a recurring problem, or show how to review journal evidence.

Required: clear promise, quick answer, structured explanation, example, limitations, practical next step, sources, metadata, and internal-link plan.

## Glossary

A glossary page owns definition intent for one term. It should define the term, show why it matters, give a simple example, explain common confusion, identify firm-specific variation, and point to a deeper article when useful.

Required: one-sentence definition, plain explanation, example, related terms, source notes, and reviewed links.

## Separation rule

The glossary defines. The blog teaches application. When both exist, they must complement each other and use different titles, promises, and primary intents.
