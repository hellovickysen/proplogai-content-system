# ProplogAI Content Principles

## We teach, we don't impress.

Simple language beats complex language.

---

## Discipline over profits.

Every article reinforces process over outcome.

---

## Every concept needs an example.

Don't explain.
Demonstrate.

---

## Every article answers beginner questions before advanced ones.

---

## Every article creates confidence, not hype.

---

## Never promise success.

Teach better decisions.

---

## Every article should leave the reader able to take one practical action today.

---

## Use visuals only when they improve understanding.

---

## Promote ProplogAI naturally.

The product should feel like the logical next step, not an advertisement.
