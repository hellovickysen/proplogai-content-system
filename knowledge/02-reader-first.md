# Reader-First Teaching

Write for a motivated beginner who may understand basic trading but not prop-firm rule mechanics, journal metrics, risk formulas, or behavioural terminology.

Use this order:

1. Answer the main question.
2. Explain why it matters.
3. Define the term in plain English.
4. Show a labelled example with assumptions.
5. Explain limitations and firm-specific variation.
6. Give a practical review step.

Aim for Grade 6-8 readability without deleting necessary technical meaning. Use short paragraphs and one idea per section. Define terms at first use and link to one relevant glossary page when it genuinely helps.

Do not assume that a profitable trade followed the plan or that a losing trade broke it. Separate process quality from outcome.
