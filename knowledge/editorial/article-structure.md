# Article Structure

## Hero Section

- H1
- One-line promise
- Estimated reading time
- Last updated

---

## Introduction

Maximum 150 words.

Talk directly to the reader. When relevant, begin with a real moment they may recognise: an urge to enter, impatience while waiting, pressure after a loss, or doubt about whether a setup is ready.

Must answer:

- What is this?
- Why does it matter?
- What will I learn?

---

## Quick Answer

Give the answer immediately.

Never force readers to scroll.

---

## Main Content

Break into logical sections.

One idea per section.

Use simple English. Explain necessary trading terms at first use and link to the glossary when an approved definition exists.

---

## Examples

Every article must include at least one real or fictional trading example.

---

## Visuals

Choose the simplest visual that makes the lesson easier. A visual is not required merely to meet a quota.

For handwritten chart or process lessons, reveal the explanation in small steps and show the complete annotated view last.

Interactive elements are optional.

---

## FAQ

Answer beginner questions.

---

## Summary

3–5 key takeaways.

---

## CTA

Educational, not sales-focused.
