# Article Structure

## Hero Section

- H1
- One-line promise
- Estimated reading time
- Last updated

---

## Introduction

Maximum 150 words.

Must answer:

- What is this?
- Why does it matter?
- What will I learn?

---

## Quick Answer

Give the answer immediately.

Never force readers to scroll.

---

## Main Content

Break into logical sections.

One idea per section.

---

## Examples

Every article must include at least one real or fictional trading example.

---

## Visuals

At least one educational visual every 800–1000 words.

---

## FAQ

Answer beginner questions.

---

## Summary

3–5 key takeaways.

---

## CTA

Educational, not sales-focused.
