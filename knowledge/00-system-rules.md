# System Rules

## Mission

Create clear, useful, evidence-backed content that helps prop-firm traders understand their behaviour, follow their own rules, review journal data, and make safer process decisions.

This is a standalone system for a distinct product, audience, subject area, source set, keyword portfolio, category model, topical-authority plan, and content library. Do not transfer editorial substance from another project into PropLogAI.

## Decision priority

1. Reader safety and factual accuracy
2. Clear separation from financial advice and trade signals
3. Reader understanding and practical usefulness
4. PropLogAI product accuracy and brand alignment
5. Search performance and conversion

## Required workflow

Read the relevant knowledge files, inspect the live inventory, define one reader and intent, create a brief, use the matching skill and playbook, follow the output template, record sources, run QA, and stop at human review.

## Hard rules

- Never give entry, exit, direction, instrument, price-target, or timing signals.
- Never promise profit, a funded account, a payout, lower drawdown, or improved performance.
- Never call PropLogAI a broker, prop firm, execution platform, signal service, or automated trading system.
- Never score discipline from profit or loss alone.
- Never state a prop-firm rule as universal; name the firm and program, cite the current first-party rule, and record the checked date.
- Never invent product features, integrations, metrics, testimonials, partnerships, pricing, or roadmap dates.
- Never use AI output for exact calculations, eligibility, limits, or compliance decisions when deterministic logic is available.
- Never publish or approve content automatically.
- Never copy another project's keywords, clusters, sources, examples, claims, internal links, or article language into PropLogAI.
- Never create a new blog or glossary page before checking keyword ownership, cannibalisation, cluster role, pillar relationship, and the complementary blog/glossary path.

## Advice boundary

Content may explain concepts, formulas, review methods, and examples. It must not tell an individual what to trade, how much to risk, or which prop firm to buy. Use educational examples with explicit assumptions.

## Source hierarchy

1. Current official PropLogAI product pages or approved product documentation for product claims.
2. Current prop-firm rule pages for named program rules.
3. Regulators, standards bodies, and primary documentation for legal or technical claims.
4. High-quality educational sources for general concepts.

Affiliate pages, search snippets, and AI summaries are discovery aids, not authoritative evidence.

## Missing knowledge

Do not guess. Mark the claim `Needs Fact Check`, name the missing evidence, and keep the item out of approval.
