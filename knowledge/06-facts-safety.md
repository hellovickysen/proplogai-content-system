# Facts and Safety

## Claims that require current verification

- PropLogAI features, pricing, trials, integrations, data handling, and availability
- Prop-firm rules, limits, payout conditions, evaluation stages, and account terms
- Laws, regulations, tax treatment, and platform policies
- Market statistics, benchmark ranges, and performance claims
- Formulas whose implementation varies by firm

Record the source URL, publisher, checked date, claim supported, and any limitation.

Before drafting or approval, map each sensitive claim to the applicable register in `content-map/source-registers/`. Follow `knowledge/11-source-expiry-rules.md`. A missing, expired, `Needs Fact Check`, or `Prohibited` row blocks the claim.

## Financial-safety checks

- Education is clearly separated from personalised advice.
- Examples do not recommend a risk percentage or trading action.
- Firm-specific rules are labelled and dated.
- Outcomes are not framed as guaranteed or controllable.
- Risk and uncertainty are visible.
- No signal, forecast, or profit promise appears.

## Product checks

Use current first-party PropLogAI evidence. If a feature cannot be verified, omit it or mark it for product-owner review.
