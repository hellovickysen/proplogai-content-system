# Quality Standard

A publishable item must score at least 90/100 overall. Accuracy and Safety must each score at least 9/10. Any unsupported product, rule, legal, profit, performance, eligibility, signal, forecast, or personalized-risk claim is an automatic fail.

Score ten categories from 0–10 using `checklists/blog-quality-checklist.md`: Accuracy, Safety, Beginner Clarity, Educational Value, Structure, SEO, Visual Learning, Interactive Learning, Internal Linking, and PropLogAI Alignment.

Human approval of the exact revision is required after AI QA.

For blogs, compare the complete reading experience with the approved reference article at `/blogs/overtrading-prop-firm-challenges` and `knowledge/12-direct-trader-teaching.md`. The comparison is about reader usefulness, direct trader voice, simple explanation, practical examples, glossary integration, visual teaching, accuracy, and safety. It does not require the same headings, example, visual count, or interaction.
