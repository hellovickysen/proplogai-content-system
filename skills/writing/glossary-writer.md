# Glossary Writer

## Responsibility

Create or refresh one glossary term from an approved glossary brief.

## Required shape

One-sentence definition, plain-language explanation, why it matters, labelled example, common confusion, related terms, deeper guide where useful, sources, and checked date.

Use `/glossary/overtrading` and `knowledge/13-glossary-teaching-standard.md` as the approved quality benchmark. Give the definition immediately, then talk calmly to one trader in very simple English. Use a concrete trading example only when it improves understanding. Choose visuals for teaching value; interactive elements are optional.

Do not give universal percentages or thresholds when firms differ. Do not turn the term into a thin sales page. Keep definition intent distinct from blog intent.

Save to `content/drafts/glossary/` with status `Draft Generated`.
