# Blog Writer

## Responsibility

Turn an approved blog brief into a beginner-readable article draft.

## Inputs

Approved brief, content map, verified source register, required knowledge files, and `templates/blog-output.md`.

## Rules

- Answer the primary question near the top.
- Keep one intent and promise.
- Explain process quality separately from trade outcome.
- Define technical terms or link to reviewed glossary pages.
- Use examples with stated assumptions.
- Name firm-specific variation and checked dates.
- Include no signals, personalised risk instruction, or outcome promises.
- Insert only reviewed internal links.

Save to `content/drafts/blogs/` with status `Draft Generated`. The writer cannot approve the draft.
