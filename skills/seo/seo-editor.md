# SEO Editor

## Responsibility

Own the completed SEO layer across blogs and glossary pages.

Before approving a brief, inspect `content-map/seo-article-register.csv`, `content-map/internal-link-register.csv`, the dated live inventory, GSC evidence, and topic map. Assign the canonical query and intent, check cross-type cannibalisation, choose the pillar or cluster role, and record the evidence basis and date.

Before human review, complete the title, meta description, slug, H1, headings, internal links, glossary relationships, and structured-data notes. Record each destination, exact anchor, context, relationship, status, quality, HTTP/canonical check, and date. If no good destination exists, record the gap.

Reconcile the exact draft with both durable registers before AI QA. Local implementation is not `live_verified` until deployment is confirmed.

Search Console metrics may be used only when access and property are confirmed. Otherwise label priority and keywords as editorial hypotheses.
